    Onchain AML: Crypto risk analysis

    // 3 months | UX Design

    // 2 Designers + 1 P0 + 3 Front-End Engineers

    Intro

    Onchain AML: A platform that helps compliance officers analyze the risks associated with cryptocurrency entities and addresses by visualizing transaction flows and address information.

    It consists of 3 parts: Know your transaction (KYT), Know your address (KYA), Know your entity (KYE)

    Know Your Transaction (KYT)

    Interviewed 5 compliance officers to draw out the user journey and identify the problems .

    Know Your Entity (KYE)

    Verifying the identity and legitimacy of a cryptocurrency exchange to ensure safe trading.

    Know Your Address (KYA)

    Multi-hop transaction for address analysis
